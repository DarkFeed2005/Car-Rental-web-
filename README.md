## 🌟 Overview

KpolitX Car Rental is a premium vehicle rental platform designed specifically for Sri Lankan travelers. This Single Page Application (SPA) provides an intuitive and seamless booking experience with interactive vehicle selection, dynamic pricing, and comprehensive fleet management across multiple brands.

### Why KpolitX Car Rental?

- **Diverse Fleet**: 15+ premium vehicles from Tesla, Toyota, Honda, Mazda, and Mitsubishi
- **Modern UI/UX**: Smooth animations with scroll-reveal effects
- **Mobile-First**: Fully responsive design optimized for all devices
- **Smart Navigation**: Glassmorphic navbar with dynamic color transitions
- **Trusted Service**: 100% trusted platform with verified brands

## 🚀 Usage

### Browsing Vehicles

1. **Home Page**:
   - View the hero section with featured vehicles
   - Read about the trusted platform benefits

2. **Search & Filter**:
   - Enter your pick-up location
   - Select pick-up date and time
   - Choose return date and time
   - Click "Search" to find available vehicles

3. **Explore Catalog**:
   - Click brand tabs (Tesla, Mitsubishi, Mazda, Toyota, Honda)
   - View vehicle specifications and pricing
   - Check ratings and reviews
   - Compare different models

4. **Vehicle Details**:
   - Passenger capacity
   - Transmission type (Manual/Autopilot)
   - Fuel efficiency or electric range
   - Fuel type (Electric/Diesel)
   - Daily rental rate

5. **Book Your Ride**:
   - Click "Rent Now" on your chosen vehicle
   - Complete booking form
   - Receive confirmation

### Navigation Features

- **Smooth Scrolling**: Click nav links to jump to sections
- **Mobile Menu**: Tap hamburger icon on mobile devices
- **Subscribe**: Enter email for latest updates
- **Social Connect**: Follow on social media
